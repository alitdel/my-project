Say No To Drugs

Drug abuse can harm health, education, relationships, and future opportunities. Awareness, support, and prevention are the first steps to a safer community.

Get Support Learn More

Common drugs and substances abused

Marijuana

Often used recreationally but can impair memory, motivation, and lung health.

Heroin

A highly addictive opioid that can lead to overdose, infection, and long-term damage.

Cocaine

A stimulant that raises heart rate and blood pressure while damaging mental health.

Methamphetamine

Causes extreme dependency, tooth decay, weight loss, and serious behavior changes.

Effects of Drug Abuse

Drug abuse affects the whole person — physical health, mental wellbeing, family life, and long-term goals.

  • School dropout and lost opportunity.
  • Chronic health conditions like heart disease and lung problems.
  • Impaired judgment, risky behavior, and accidents.
  • Relationship breakdown, homelessness, and crime.
  • Financial instability and legal consequences.

Prevention and support

Effective prevention is built on education, strong support systems, and easy access to treatment.

  • Education: Accurate information helps people make healthier decisions.
  • Support networks: Family, friends, and community groups reduce peer pressure.
  • Community programs: Safe activities and outreach keep people engaged.
  • Treatment access: Counseling and recovery services help individuals stay sober.
