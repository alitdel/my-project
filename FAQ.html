Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most important questions about drug awareness, recovery, and how to find help.

What are the first signs of drug abuse?

Common signs include sudden mood changes, withdrawal from family or friends, poor personal hygiene, and changes in sleeping or eating habits.

How can I help someone who is struggling?

Offer support without judgement, encourage professional help, and connect them with trusted resources or support groups.

Where can I get immediate help?

Contact local hotlines, health clinics, or community support centers. If in danger, call emergency services immediately.

Can drug addiction be treated?

Yes. Treatment options include counseling, medical support, recovery programs, peer support groups, and long-term follow-up care.

How do I join a recovery program?

Speak with a healthcare professional or counselor who can recommend programs based on your location, needs, and level of support.

Is it safe to talk to someone anonymously?

Yes. Many hotlines and support services provide anonymous help so you can get advice without sharing personal details.
