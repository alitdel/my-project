Our Services

💬 Anonymous Counseling

We help individuals addicted to drugs recover through counseling and support services.

📚 Training and Education

Training the youth about dangers of drug abuse and promoting healthy lifestyle choices.

🏠 Rehabilitation

We offer ongoing support and consultation to help individuals recover from drug addiction.

📝 Receiving Report Cases of Drug Abuse

We receive and document cases of drug abuse reported by the community.

📊 Update Tracking of Drug Abuse Cases

We regularly update and track drug abuse cases in various areas to monitor trends and allocate resources effectively.

🚨 Emergency Services

We provide emergency support and resources for individuals in crisis due to drug abuse.
